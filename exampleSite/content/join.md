## Receive

Write to nightpost@example.com with the word *letters* in the subject line. Letters arrive when they are ready, usually at night. There is no charge, and no mailing list you cannot leave.

## Write back

Correspondence is welcome. A good reply does not need to be long. Tell us where you were when you read, what the weather was doing, or which sentence you have been thinking about since.

## What we cannot accept

- Anything forwarded without the permission of the person who wrote it.
- Schedules, deadlines, and requests for content.
- Photographs of people who did not agree to be in them.
