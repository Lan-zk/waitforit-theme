---
title: "The Lamp Left On"
description: "On the third floor of a house I have never entered, there is a lamp that stays lit long after the harbour goes quiet."
date: 2026-03-14
---

The lamp on the third floor of the house across the lane has been on for eleven nights. I know because I count them the way other people count ships. It is a small lamp, the kind you set beside a bed if you expect to fall asleep reading, and it goes out a little after four, when the harbour begins to sound like itself again.

I have decided it belongs to someone who cannot sleep, and that is the end of my detective work. I could walk over and knock. I have not. There is a difference between a light that is waiting for someone and a light that is simply being kept, and I would rather not find out which one this is.

What I wanted to note down is that I have started leaving my own lamp on. Not for anyone. It seems unfair that one house should do all the keeping. So at eleven I turn the little brass switch and go to bed, and the lane has two lights in it instead of one, and the dark between them looks a little more like a street.

The fog came in low on the twelfth night and the lamps looked like lanterns hanging in nothing. I walked home along the water instead of the road, which is a thing I only do when the weather is like that. Nothing happened. Nobody was waiting. It was, in a small way, the best evening of the month.
