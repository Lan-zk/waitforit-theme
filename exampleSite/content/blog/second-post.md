---
title: "The Drawer of Unfinished Notes"
description: "Some notes are written only to be folded twice and put away."
date: 2026-07-01
---

There is a drawer in the desk that holds the notes I did not finish. It is not a sad drawer. It is simply a drawer, and it has a certain smell of paper and old brass, and I open it perhaps once a month to make sure the thin ones have not slipped through the back.

Some notes are written only to be folded twice and put away. This is one of them. I am writing it because the thing I wanted to say would not sit still until it had been said on paper, and because saying it to the room is not the same as saying it to you.

Last week the fog came in so low that the street lamps looked like lanterns hanging in nothing. I walked home along the water instead of the road, which is a thing I only do when the weather is like that. Nothing happened. Nobody was waiting. It was, in a small way, the best evening of the month.

If you ever find this, you will know more about me than you asked to. That seems fair.

I will not finish it. I am leaving it here anyway.
