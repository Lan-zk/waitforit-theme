A collection of stylesheet fragments and typographic defaults for long-form reading: one fixed frame, two voices, hairlines instead of boxes, and no accent hue.

The kit is deliberately small. It assumes a static generator and a self-hosted OFL serif; everything else is a decision the site makes once.
