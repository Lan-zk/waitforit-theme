A build-only image pipeline for per-page social cards: 1200×630, a shared paper texture, serif titles, and character-aware wrapping for Chinese and English.

The two TrueType faces used for rendering are build assets. They never ship as web fonts, and the build needs no browser, Node process, or network access.
