---
title: "Weather Log, Tuesday"
description: "A short note on fog, rope, and the order of small things."
date: 2026-09-30
---

Fog until eleven, then a thin sun that did not reach the stones. The harbour sounded farther away than it looked, which happens when the air is wet enough to carry the buoy bell sideways.

Two boats came in late and one did not come in at all. Nobody is worried; it is tied at the far dock and its owner is asleep. These are the facts. I write them down because the order of small things is the only order I am good at keeping.
