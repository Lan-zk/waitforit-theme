---
title: "Two Chairs by the Window"
description: "A short note on the furniture of waiting."
date: 2026-10-05
---

There are two chairs by the window of the reading room and only one of them is ever occupied. The other holds a coat, or a bag, or nothing, depending on the hour. I have started to think of it as the chair for the part of the day that has not arrived yet.

The librarian does not move it. Neither do I. Some furniture is a decision about how to wait, and this one has already been made.
