## A quiet place for letters

The Night Post is a small correspondence project. It publishes letters that were written slowly, at night, and sent without any especial hope of an answer. Some of them were posted. Most of them simply exist.

## Why letters

- A letter has to be addressed to someone, even if that someone is only the version of yourself who will read it later.
- A letter takes the time it takes. There is no way to scroll past the middle of one and still call it read.
- A letter can be kept. Anything you can keep becomes a small argument against forgetting.

## What this is not

It is not a magazine, a feed, or a platform. There is no subscriber count, no comment section, and nothing to catch up on. If a letter is late, it was late when it was written, and that is part of it.
