## Slowness

We publish when a letter is ready, not according to a schedule. Some months there are two. Some months there is nothing, and the archive is meant to be enough on those nights.

## Privacy

Letters are shared only with the person they were addressed to, and with the archive. There are no trackers here, no analytics, and no third parties. We ask the same restraint of our readers: nothing here is meant to travel faster than it can be read.

## Attention

A letter asks to be read once, slowly. Everything on this site is arranged around that request: one column, generous margins, no interruptions.

- No pop-ups, no banners, no request to subscribe before you can read.
- No infinite list. The archive ends, politely.
- No images where a sentence would do.
